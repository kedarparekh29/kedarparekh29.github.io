LifeVerse Privacy Policy

LifeVerse: Life Simulator, by Capsule Studios · Effective 6 October 2026

The short version
  • You don't make an account, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your lives, characters and settings are saved on your phone. We don't receive a copy.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob collects device data to show and measure ads. Where the law gives you a choice about personalised ads, the game asks you, and you can change your answer later.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.

1. Who we are

LifeVerse: Life Simulator ("LifeVerse", "the game") is made and published by Capsule Studios ("we", "us"), based in India. For anything about this policy or your data, email kedarparekh0104@gmail.com.

2. What the game keeps on your phone

To let you continue where you left off, the game stores on your device:

  • your game progress: the lives you play, the characters you create (including any names you type), and your history and records across lives;
  • your settings, such as sound and music;
  • a record of what you have bought, so it stays unlocked.

This stays on your device and is not sent to us. If you have Android's backup turned on, Android may include it in your device backup to your Google account; that backup is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Ads, and what Google AdMob collects

The free game shows ads served by Google AdMob, a Google service. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect:

  • device identifiers, including your Android advertising ID;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location (city or region, not precise location);
  • information about your device and the app, such as device model, operating system version and language;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic information, such as ad loading errors.

Google uses this to deliver ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same one, and prevent fraud, and to personalise ads only where you have allowed it. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. Residents of US states with privacy laws can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for ads. In either case you can change your choice at any time from Privacy settings in the game's menu.
  • Your advertising ID. You can reset or delete it in your phone's settings (on most phones: Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Ads suitable for teens. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for a teen audience.
  • Remove Ads. Buying Remove Ads (or LifeVerse Plus) turns off banners and interstitial ads. Reward videos you choose to watch are still provided by AdMob.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your card number, billing address or other payment details. Google tells the game what you bought, along with a purchase reference, so the game can unlock it and restore it if you reinstall or change phones. Payments are covered by Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't require or offer accounts or logins.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools besides AdMob.
  • The game doesn't ask for your precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • The game has no chat. Nothing you type is shared with other players.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

6. Children

LifeVerse is designed for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email us and we will help remove it.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Game data stays on your phone until you clear it (Settings → Apps → LifeVerse → Storage → Clear data) or uninstall the game. Because we don't hold a copy, we can't restore lost progress for you, and there is nothing on our side to delete.
  • Purchases are recorded by Google Play and can be restored on a new device signed in to the same Google account.
  • Ad data is kept by Google under its own retention policies. Resetting or deleting your advertising ID breaks the link to past ad activity.

8. Security

Game data is protected by Android's app sandbox, which stops other apps reading it. Data exchanged with Google for ads and purchases is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the EU and UK GDPR, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain. Because the game keeps your data on your device, most of these you can exercise directly: clear the app's data, change your choices in Privacy settings, or reset your advertising ID. For anything else, including complaints and grievances, email kedarparekh0104@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days, and where a request concerns data held by Google, we will point you to the right place.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above. If a change affects what is collected or how it is used, we'll also tell you in the game.
